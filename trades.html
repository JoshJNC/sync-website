The questions everyone asks

I'm not technical. Is this going to be a nightmare?

No. You sign in with the Google or Microsoft account you already have, tick which inbox to watch, and you're done. If you'd rather not do it alone, I'll set it up with you on a fifteen-minute call and you can watch me do it.

Do I have to change my email address?

Not at all. You keep the same address and the same inbox. Sy-nc sits behind it and writes drafts into the inbox you already open every day.

Does it send things without me seeing them?

Only if you tell it to. Out of the box, every reply and every follow-up is a draft waiting for you to read and send. If you get comfortable and want the chasing to go out on its own, there's a switch for that — and you can turn it straight back off.

Is my email private?

You connect through Google's and Microsoft's own secure sign-in, so we never see or store your password, and you can disconnect in one click at any time. What we do with your data is set out plainly in the privacy policy.

What if it turns out not to be for me?

Then you stop. There's no contract, no notice period and nothing to send back. Twenty-one days is enough to know either way.

Does it work for my trade?

If people email or message you asking for a price, it works. Builders, electricians, plumbers, roofers, gardeners, cleaners, groundworkers, installers — the problem is the same one in every case.
